### Structured Abstract
**Background**: Sepsis is a leading cause of hospital mortality. Existing machine learning alert systems cause severe alarm fatigue due to poor positive predictive value (PPV).
**Methods**: We developed BioMarkerX, a strictly causal two-tier risk prediction framework evaluated on 16,000+ ICU patients. We introduced vasopressor-adjusted MAP gating, length-of-stay adaptive window persistence, and machine-zero SHAP attributions.
**Results**: In external validation on 5,999 reserved patients, BioMarkerX achieved AUROC 0.8658, AUPRC 0.1690, 99.63% Patient Sensitivity, and 52.34%–61.29% Confirmation PPV with 36.0h median lead time.
**Conclusion**: BioMarkerX eliminates false alarm fatigue while capturing 99.6% of sepsis events, offering a transformative tool for ICU clinical decision support.
